# Caption Design

- Use a font with broad language coverage and a clear bold weight.
- Use a maximum of two lines and short phrase groups.
- Maintain high contrast with a subtle shadow or restrained backing plate.
- Keep captions away from the bottom app controls and top interface overlays.
- Use consistent baseline, margins, line spacing, and animation.
- Highlight only key words; keep the rest stable.
- Proofread ASR output, especially names, numbers, acronyms, and code-switching.
- Provide an SRT/VTT sidecar when the user may need editable captions.
