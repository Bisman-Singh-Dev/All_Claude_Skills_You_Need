# Video Quality & Lighting Skill

A Claude skill for natural, professional video enhancement: exposure, lighting, color, noise reduction, sharpness, upscaling, and export quality.

## Install
1. Extract `video-quality-lighting.zip`.
2. In Claude, open **Customize → Skills** (or the Skills area available in your account).
3. Upload the extracted `video-quality-lighting` folder or its `SKILL.md`, depending on the interface.
4. Enable the skill.

The skill is designed to guide Claude through inspecting footage first, making restrained corrections, and checking the final result for artifacts.
